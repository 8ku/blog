Today’s question: **When was the last time you changed your mind about something important? What made you change?**

<!--more-->

I can’t say wether it was an important changed, but something recently changed my mind.

For about five years, I’ve wanted to leave this country. Since then, I’ve tried to avoid becoming more tied to it. For example, I avoided using its most popular apps and looking for work here because I didn’t want to build a future in this country.

Recently, though, I realized that my driver’s license would expire in two months. At first, I decided not to renew it because I didn’t expect to use it in the future.

When I told one of my friends, she persuaded me to renew it anyway, just as a precaution. She said that getting a new driver’s license has become much more difficult these days, so if renewing it wasn’t too troublesome, it would be worth doing.

I was persuaded, and in the end I renewed my license. Afterward, I felt that something inside me had change, although I couldn’t really explain what it was.
