Q: **Do you think people usually do the right thing because they are good people, or because the situation makes it easy to do the right thing?**
<!--more-->

I don’t believe there are truly good people in the world. I think people behave differently depending on the situation.

In fact, I think doing the right thing is usually harder than doing wrong thing. From my experience, admitting a mistake at work is one of the hardest things people can do, and that’s something I really dislike.

I want to meet people who are honest and sincere, but reality isn’t that ideal.

And I think the Ideas of “good” and “bad” are ethical standards that depend on social environment and on a person’s values. In other words, they depend on what people believe.

Moreover, how can we really say whether a person is good or bad? If someone has done 100 good things but also 99 bad things, can we still call them a good person? Or to take a more extreme example, what if someone has done 100 good things and only one bad thing?

And what about someone who has killed many innocent people but is also a loving father? Should we call that person good or bad?

Further question: **If every person’s behavior depends heavily on the situation, should we judge people less harshly—or should we judge them just as harshly because they still made a choice?**
