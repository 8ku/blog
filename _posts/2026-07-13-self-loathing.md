Q: **If you could erase one part of your personality forever, would you do it? Why or why not?**  
<!--more-->

Unexpectedly, I can accept every part of my personality now. I don’t want to erase any of it. Maybe a few years ago, I could have named one or two traits that I wanted to get rid of. But at this moment, I’m satisfied with who I am.

I still want to change, not by changing my personality though. What I want to change is my environment and my career. I also believe that human being are capable of learning and changing, so who I am today is not the final version of myself. If my life changes, my personality may change as a consequence.
