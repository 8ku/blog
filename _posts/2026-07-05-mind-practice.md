Q: **Have you ever met someone you disliked at first, but later came to respect? What changed your opinion?**

My old colleague is someone I disliked when we worked together. I was ux designer and she was a ui designer. She was slow to understand things. At the time, I often felt that I had to explain everything we were going to do, which made me feel exhausted.

After I left the company, she invited me to hang out several times. For a long time, I kept turning her down. But she didn’t give up. She kept asking, and eventually I felt guilty about refusing her over and over again, so I agreed to meet her.

Unexpectedly, she was easier to talk to outside the workplace. We talked about art, life, our feeling about the world, and the tv shows we had been watching recently. We often had different perspectives, but overall it was a pleasure experience.

I wouldn’t use the word “respect “ to describe how I feel now, but it definitely changed the way I see her as a person.
